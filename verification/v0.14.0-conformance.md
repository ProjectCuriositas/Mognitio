The acceptance-boundary seam reports a post-acceptance kind failure without
introducing a production reopen. Owned getdents64 ENOTDIR remains internal.
The native scratch fault first performs real unmap, then replaces its result.
Fixtures do not mistake process teardown for observed resource release.
